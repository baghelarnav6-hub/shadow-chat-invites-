Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where can I download ShadowChat?

Use the official download page. It links to the Android APK and the matching GitHub release page as a fallback.

How do I join a group?

Open the invitation link shared by a group member on your Android device. A valid link opens ShadowChat's join flow.

What if the download does not start?

On the download page, choose “Open official release page” and tap the APK listed under Assets. Android may ask you to allow your browser to install apps from this source.

Is ShadowChat available on iPhone?

The verified download currently available here is an Android APK. iPhone availability has not been published.
